# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

TimeKeeper is a small open-source project. Contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Provide clear, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent when possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Posting private information without permission
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block users when necessary to keep the project healthy.
